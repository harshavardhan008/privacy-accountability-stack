How to read this honestly

These probes were written by the author of the gateway, and each trial changes the synthetic customer, not the mechanism. The intervals describe this test set. They say nothing about how common these failures are in real deployments. The exam shows that the controls do what they claim and where they stop, which is what a reviewer needs to judge them. The checkup is how the real-world numbers get collected.

The text scanner is a deliberately simple baseline (emails and phone numbers). A scanner with name recognition would catch more, though it would still not know whose data it is or what the purpose was.
